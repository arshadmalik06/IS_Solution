2. Mandatory Certification Scheme

Scheme-II (CRS)

For manufacturing and commercializing 9W and 12W self-ballasted LED lamps for general lighting in India, statutory compliance is mandated under the Electronics and Information Technology Goods (Compulsory Registration Order) administered by BIS.

Registration is compulsory under Scheme-II (CRS). CRS Mark with unique R-XXXXXXXX number and standard statement is legally enforceable before customs or domestic distribution.
